Colours
Colours usage
The palette is built around a neutral
foundation — near-black for primary text,
light greys for backgrounds — with a set of
deep, earthy accent colours used sparingly
to add character without overwhelming.

Accent colours should appear in roughly
20% of any composition, and work best in
dithered imagery, gradient dots, and
selective UI highlights. Avoid using more
than two accent colours in a single context.

The accent family carries the primary brand
personality. Never use them as large
background fills — they're meant to
punctuate, not dominate.

Imagery
Imagery usage
All imagery is generated in Midjourney and
composited or animated in Unicorn Studio
before use on site or in social content.

Images draw from two visual registers:
painterly illustration and hyperrealistic
photography. Both are valid as they will be
edited.

Across both styles, keep the following
consistent: medieval and chess themes
(knights, castles, strategy), earthy and muted
tones with occasional green, natural light
sources (candles, sunlight through forest),
and a sense of scale and gravitas. Avoid
anything cartoonish, overly colourful, or
modern in setting.
